-- The reef, as WGSL: three sources.
--
--   GROUND  a pass drawn once (and again when the box changes size): the
--           still scenery (sand, boulders, corals), kept per art pixel as
--           light, material, depth and warm light.
--   LIVE    a pass drawn every frame, at one texel per art pixel: what moves
--           (fish, turtle, manta, jellyfish, kelp, shafts, snow, bubbles)
--           over GROUND's pixels, lit, cut to hard pixels and snapped to the
--           palette.
--   SHOW    the layer shader that scales LIVE up, nearest-neighbour.
--
-- The look follows splats.cobanov.dev's pixel-art gaussian splats: forms
-- sculpted in 3D, each surface point lit and coloured through a ramp, the
-- picture drawn small, translucent things cut to on/off pixels by a Bayer
-- threshold, every pixel the nearest of a small palette. The scene and its
-- models are this plugin's own. Generated by src/build.py from src/*.wgsl:
-- edit those, not this file.
--
-- params[0] = { -, -, dither, speed }
-- params[1] = { alpha, brightness, zoom, lift }
-- params[2] = { open water's alpha, how muted (0 to 1), -, - }
